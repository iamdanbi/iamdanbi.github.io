Clean Block

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026 · Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Chasoori Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the mobile game Clean Block (the “Game”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use Clean Block.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate, improve, analyze, and support the Game and its advertising features.

1. Information We Collect

Clean Block does not require you to create an account.

We do not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or email address in order to play the Game.

However, certain information may be collected or processed automatically through third-party services integrated into the Game, including advertising and analytics services.

This information may include:

  • device type and operating system;
  • device and application identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers, where permitted;
  • IP address or approximate location derived from an IP address;
  • app version and technical information;
  • gameplay events and interactions;
  • session information;
  • advertising interactions and related information;
  • crash, diagnostic, or performance information; and
  • consent and privacy preference information.

The exact information processed may vary depending on your device, operating system, region, privacy settings, and consent choices.

2. Advertising

Clean Block uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information about your device and your interaction with advertisements for purposes such as:

  • displaying advertisements;
  • measuring advertising performance;
  • limiting repeated advertisements;
  • detecting and preventing fraud and abuse; and
  • providing personalized advertising where legally permitted and where the required consent or permission has been obtained.

Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Clean Block currently displays banner advertisements. We may introduce additional advertising formats in future versions of the Game, including optional rewarded advertisements that provide in-game benefits in exchange for voluntarily viewing an advertisement.

For more information about how Google processes information, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent Management

Clean Block uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) where appropriate to obtain and manage privacy choices required under applicable privacy laws and regulations.

Depending on your location, you may be presented with options concerning how your information may be used for advertising and related purposes.

Where required, you may also be provided with a way to review or change applicable privacy choices from within the Game.

4. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On Apple devices, Clean Block follows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) requirements.

Where required, the Game will request your permission before accessing Apple’s advertising identifier or engaging in tracking that requires permission under Apple’s policies.

If you select “Ask App Not to Track,” you may continue to use the Game. Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from playing Clean Block.

Your ATT choice does not necessarily prevent all data processing. Information that does not constitute tracking under Apple’s policies may still be processed for purposes such as analytics, security, fraud prevention, and providing the Game, subject to applicable laws and your other privacy choices.

5. Analytics

Clean Block uses GameAnalytics to help us understand how the Game is used and improve gameplay, performance, balance, and the overall user experience.

GameAnalytics may process information such as:

  • gameplay events;
  • game progression and scores;
  • session information;
  • device and operating system information;
  • app version;
  • technical and performance information; and
  • identifiers or other information used to distinguish game installations or sessions, as permitted by applicable laws and platform policies.

We use analytics information primarily to understand aggregate usage patterns, identify problems, and improve Clean Block.

For information about GameAnalytics’ privacy practices, please refer to GameAnalytics’ Privacy Policy.

6. Information Stored Locally

Certain information may be stored locally on your device to provide Game functionality.

This may include information such as:

  • high scores;
  • game progress;
  • sound preferences;
  • haptic preferences; and
  • other Game settings.

Information stored only locally on your device is not transmitted to Chasoori Games unless a feature or third-party service specifically requires such transmission.

Deleting the Game may remove locally stored information.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with Clean Block may be used to:

  • provide and operate the Game;
  • maintain Game functionality;
  • save preferences and progress;
  • understand how players interact with the Game;
  • improve gameplay and user experience;
  • diagnose technical issues;
  • measure Game performance;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, or security problems;
  • comply with legal obligations; and
  • enforce applicable policies.

8. Third-Party Services

Clean Block currently integrates third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob — advertising;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — privacy and consent management; and
  • GameAnalytics — game analytics.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Chasoori Games does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party service providers. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Data Retention

Chasoori Games does not maintain user accounts or a separate database containing personal profiles of Clean Block players.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the retention policies of those service providers.

Locally stored Game information generally remains on your device until it is removed by the Game, reset by you where such functionality is available, or deleted when the Game or its associated data is removed.

10. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Because Clean Block does not currently require user accounts, we generally cannot identify an individual player solely from ordinary gameplay.

If you have questions or requests concerning privacy or information processed directly by Chasoori Games, you may contact us using the email address below.

For information controlled independently by third-party services such as Google or GameAnalytics, you may need to exercise your privacy rights directly through those providers.

11. Children’s Privacy

Clean Block is designed for a general audience.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to Chasoori Games.

Advertising and analytics services used by the Game may be subject to additional requirements concerning children and younger users depending on the user’s location and applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by Clean Block may process information on servers located in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required, such providers are responsible for implementing appropriate safeguards for international data transfers in accordance with applicable law.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights concerning your personal information, such as rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Some privacy choices may be available directly through the Game, your device settings, Google services, or other third-party services.

You may contact us regarding privacy-related questions at the email address provided below.

14. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the Game to protect information under our control.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Clean Block, our practices, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
